### 2.1 AC-1: no trap types (PASS)

SPEC:898: "A case-insensitive search for `trap` in `packages/sim` and `apps/client/src` finds no class, type, enum or
entity kind with that word in it; comments, tests and the trapline exception are allowed (rule 1)."

- Test `packages/sim/tests/ac1.noTrapTypes.test.ts` parses every script and JSON file under both trees with the
  TypeScript compiler API and checks declaration names, union literals, kind-like property literals, exported names,
  file paths, and the comment-stripped text. It is not vacuous: one `it` asserts the scan sees known names
  (`CombatSim`, `WorldEvent`, `'impact'`, `'dryWood'`, `'setTuning'`), and in-memory sources prove it catches trap
  names in declarations, JSON, CSS, HTML, GLSL and file names, and skips only comments.
- Output: `AC-1: 133 files, 47300 names and kind literals, 7798 comments removed; "trap" 9× in the raw text, 0× outside comments`; `✓ tests/ac1.noTrapTypes.test.ts (10 tests)`.
- My own grep (`grep -rniI trap packages/sim/src apps/client/src`): 9 hits, all in comments (mind.ts, world.ts ×2,
  nav.ts, contacts.ts, commands.ts, panel.ts, killcam.ts, loop.ts). No `class|interface|type|enum …trap…` and no
  `kind|type|look|feature: '…trap…'`.
- Note: SPEC:898 names `packages/sim`; the test scans `packages/sim/src` (tests are allowed by the criterion anyway).

felled tree each wounding or killing a creature. Tests may script a creature's route and actions (walking a path,

creatures are scripted only by `route`, `tug`, `hold` and `charge` (allowed). The spring is detected, not caused, by

## 6. Known bugs and risks

1. **Perf: the sim step is ≈ 6 ms against SPEC:800's 5 ms.** My run: 6.54 ms median (wall 6.27 ms), two workers. Cause (ledger P38): about 31 generated thin dry
   branches (8-gon prisms, r 2-4 cm) never fall asleep on the uneven heightfield; they rock and creep millimetres a
   second (also a rule 3 problem: they twitch). Forcing them asleep for measurement drops `physics.step` from 4.2 to
   ≈ 1.3 ms. Rapier's sleep thresholds are fixed speeds; damping, CCD, facet count, friction, solver settings and
   inertia were each ruled out. **Two settle-rule attempts were reverted**: (1) put any drifting free body to sleep
   (perf 2.84 ms) broke 14 tests across AC-2(a)-(c), AC-3, AC-4 and the propped-log lab; (2) only loose bodies resting
   on terrain (perf 2.87 ms) still broke AC-2(b) (25 bearers, a stake with no impact first, bridge breaks) and AC-3
   (0/4). `settle.test.ts` keeps both seed checks as `it.fails`. Any fix changes resting bodies and so the fitted pit
   scenes. The loads memo (P38) already cut `loads.update` 3.23 → 0.68 ms with checkpoint hashes unchanged.
2. **Tile-seam drag** (known regression from the C F3 in-solver forces): a body slid by a sustained force sticks dead on
   the next 25 m heightfield tile's first edge (FIX_INTERNAL_EDGES works inside one tile). A player dragging a log
   across x or z = −75 … 75 step 25 will stick. `verbs.hands.test.ts` keeps it as `it.fails` (0.11 m/s vs ≥ 1.5).
   Fixes (one heightfield, or overlapping tiles) change the contacts the cached pit straddles.
3. **A dirt pile falls through the terrain.** In the cached pit, pile entity 15 lay at y −1,316,228 m (ledger). Cause:
   spoil poured under a resting body leaves its centre under the surface and Rapier pushes it out of the world. A fix
   (`liftBuried`) broke the pit build and was reverted. Not gated; clutter (rule 10).
4. **Planner over-dig.** The small planner pit's floor reaches −2.12 … −1.65 m against −1.5 m; the pin was loosened to
   0.7 m. The planner builds the cached pit, so a fix rebuilds the scene AC-2(b)/AC-3 rest on.
5. **Fragile traps.** The deadfall passes on fitted geometry (6/9 and 0/4 in the logged sweeps); AC-3 passes 4/4 only
   at the fitted tree (neighbours 1/4-3/4). The swinging-log tie-off holds only in a timing window. These gates prove
   the traps can be built from the rules; they don't show a player can build them reliably.
6. **AC-9 risk.** World hits on an ape are mild by design (bone m_eff): snag points 0.3-1.8 HP, a 40 kg log on a downed
   ape 0.2-0.5 HP. The stand-in passes 2/8. Bleed-outs and low-HP apes nothing finishes are the likely human failures.
7. **Rule 5 rests on DungeonStyle's stamina** (AC-4 control: 40.5 HP/s with stamina held at 1 vs 30 HP/s recovery).
8. **Found in this verification: the giant re-trips while rising in the pit.** In AC-3 (4/4) the state log shows
   `down/getup → down/tripped` 2-18 ticks after the rise begins, and AC-2(b) logs 3-4 `cover` trips per run in 8 of
   9 runs. Not documented in the ledger. I did not test whether the giant can ever climb out; if not, a pit is a
   permanent pin (no recovery while down, SPEC:877).
9. **AC-5 forearm speed**: 33.01 m/s during the charge, before the impact (the knuckle-charge ramp); outside the gate's
   window by the test's documented reading.
10. **Entity sleep isn't saved** (bodies reload awake, as SPEC:729 says). Saving it made loads more faithful but moved
    AC-3's fitted hunt to 3/4, so it was reverted.
11. Vitest stability: the full run's "Timeout calling onTaskUpdate" was fixed by yielding between tests
    (`tests/setup/yieldBetweenTests.ts`). My full run had no such error.

   to a Web Worker (SPEC:807).

   bleed-out within 2 s of a world knock as the world's.
